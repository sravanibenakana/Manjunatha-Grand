Our Story

GOOD FOOD. WARM WELCOME.
SHRI MANJUNATHA GRAND VEG

A place for everyday meals and special gatherings.

Shri Manjunatha Grand Veg is a vegetarian restaurant on Scout Camp Road in Doddaballapura, close to the railway station and next to Max Showroom.

Our menu brings together South Indian breakfast and tiffin, North Indian favourites, rice dishes and Indo-Chinese options. We welcome individuals, families and groups to enjoy a relaxed meal.

Our focus is simple: a varied vegetarian menu, comfortable dining and a friendly experience.
